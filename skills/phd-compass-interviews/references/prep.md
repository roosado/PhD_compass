# Interview preparation

Produces a prep sheet (from `assets/interview.md`) the owner can read in 15 minutes before the interview.

## 1. Refresh the target
- The group or programme page, looked at again now: members, current projects, news.
- 3–5 recent papers from the PI and group, each summarised in two lines: the question, the method, the main result, and one question the owner could ask about it.
- The position or programme as advertised: project aims, methods, requirements, funding, start date.
- The institution's doctoral set-up: duration, training requirements, teaching duties, if stated.
Every fact gets a link.

## 2. Likely questions
Take the relevant questions from `references/question-bank.md`, then add 5–8 specific to this target: its project, its methods, the owner's gaps against the ad's requirements, and anything in the owner's application a panel would probe (a change of field, a gap in dates, a short project).

## 3. Answers from real evidence
For each likely question, a short answer outline (not a script), built only from Profile facts and the application sent:
- **Experience questions:** situation → task → what the owner did → result → what they learned. One episode per answer, with a number or outcome where Profile has one.
- **Motivation questions:** the thread from the owner's background to this project, and one specific link to the group's work.
- **Technical questions:** the concepts the project relies on that the owner should be able to explain, with a pointer to a good review or textbook chapter for each (linked).
- **Gap questions:** acknowledge the gap in one sentence, then what the owner has done or will do about it.
Flag any question the owner cannot yet answer from real experience, and suggest how to handle it honestly.

## 4. The pitch
- **1 minute:** who the owner is, the strongest evidence, why this project.
- **3–5 minutes (thesis or research talk):** background → question → method → result → what it means for the PhD project.
If a presentation is required: an outline with a slide count that fits the time (about one slide per minute), the one message of each slide, and the likely follow-up questions.

## 5. Questions to ask them
Choose 4–6 the owner genuinely wants answered, specific to this group:
- supervision style and meeting frequency; how projects are defined;
- group size, and where recent PhD graduates went;
- funding duration against the typical time to finish; teaching duties; conference and training budget;
- the start date, and what happens between offer and start (contracts, visas);
- one question about a paper or project from step 1.

## 6. Logistics
Time zone conversion, link or address, the documents to have open (CV, letter, proposal, the ad), the tech check for online interviews, what to bring on site.

Done when the prep sheet has every section filled with sourced or Profile-based content, and the owner has seen the questions they can't yet answer.
