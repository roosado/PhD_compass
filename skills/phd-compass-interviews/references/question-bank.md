# Question bank

Recurring PhD interview questions, grouped by what the panel is testing. Pick the ones that fit the target and the panel. Questions the owner was actually asked are also collected in the save file's Interviews entries: check those first.

## Opening
- Tell us about yourself.
- Walk us through your CV.
- Why did you apply for this position or programme?

## Motivation and fit
- Why a PhD, and why now?
- Why this group, this institution and this country?
- What do you know about our recent work? Which paper interested you most, and why?
- What would you like to work on in the first year?
- Where do you see yourself after the PhD?

## Research experience
- Describe your thesis (or your main project): the question, your method, your result.
- What was your own contribution, as distinct from the group's?
- Describe a result that did not work. What did you do next?
- What was the hardest technical problem you solved, and how?
- How do you decide what to do when your supervisor is unavailable?

## Knowledge of the project and field
- Explain the core idea of this project to a non-specialist in two minutes.
- What are the main open problems in the field, and which matters most to you?
- How would you approach the project described in the ad? What could go wrong?
- Which methods, instruments or tools does the project need, and which have you used?

## Working style
- How do you organise your work and your data?
- Describe a time you worked in a team with different expertise.
- How do you handle feedback that you disagree with?
- How do you keep up with the literature?

## Practical
- When can you start? When will your current degree be complete?
- Do you meet the eligibility conditions (language, mobility rules, visa)?
- Do you have other applications or offers in progress?
- Do you have questions for us?

## Programme-style interviews (cohort admissions, US visit days)
- Which faculty would you like to work with, and why?
- How would you choose among labs or rotations?
- What do you expect from a PhD programme beyond research?
