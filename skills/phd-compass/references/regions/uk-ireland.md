# United Kingdom and Ireland

Structural patterns only. Stipend rates, fee levels and deadlines change yearly: look them up live and cite the page.

## Routes into a PhD (UK)
1. **Studentships in doctoral training programmes.** UK Research and Innovation (UKRI) funds cohorts through Doctoral Training Partnerships and Centres for Doctoral Training. A full award covers a stipend and tuition fees, with a fixed yearly application round.
2. **Project studentships.** A PI advertises a funded project (UKRI, university, charity or industry money) on FindAPhD, jobs.ac.uk or the university site.
3. **University and charity scholarships** for applicants who propose their own project, usually needing a supervisor's support first.
4. **Self-funded offers.** Many listings are self-funded or "competition funded" (the project competes for a studentship). Exclude them under a fully-funded rule, or record them as `maybe` with the funding question named.

## Eligibility traps
- **Fee status.** Tuition is charged at a "home" or "international" rate. EU, EEA and Swiss nationals without settled or pre-settled status under the EU Settlement Scheme are normally international. Irish citizens and people with settled or pre-settled status who meet the residence requirement are normally home. Confirm with the university's fee assessment. [House of Commons Library, home fee status in England](https://commonslibrary.parliament.uk/research-briefings/cbp-10708/), accessed 2026-09-17.
- **UKRI studentships and international candidates.** International students can hold UKRI studentships, but programmes normally limit international recruits to 30% of each year's intake. Awards pay fees at the home rate, so check whether the university waives the international difference: without a waiver, the studentship is not fully funded for an international student. [UKRI, MRC studentship eligibility](https://www.ukri.org/what-we-do/developing-people-and-skills/mrc/mrc-studentships/eligibility-for-mrc-studentships/), accessed 2026-09-17.
- **ATAS.** Research at PhD level in certain sensitive science and engineering subjects needs an Academic Technology Approval Scheme certificate. Nationals of the EU, EEA, Switzerland, Australia, Canada, Japan, New Zealand, Singapore, South Korea and the United States are exempt. [GOV.UK, ATAS](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/academic-technology-approval-scheme), accessed 2026-09-17.
- **Visa.** Applicants who are not UK or Irish citizens and have no settled status need a Student visa; the university issues the sponsorship confirmation after an unconditional offer.
- **English.** Universities set test and score requirements and their own waiver lists for degrees taught in English. Check per university.
- **Degree timing.** Offers are often conditional on finishing the current degree before the programme starts.

## What applications ask for
- CV, transcripts, references (usually two or three), a personal statement.
- A research proposal for self-proposed projects and some programmes; advertised projects often ask for a statement on the project instead.
- Doctoral training programmes usually interview shortlisted candidates.

## Calendar patterns
- Doctoral training programme rounds for an October start usually close in winter; look up each programme's date. Project studentships appear all year.
- Programmes often last 3.5 to 4 years, sometimes with a training year first. Confirm the funded duration in the ad.

## Ireland
- **Research Ireland Government of Ireland Postgraduate Scholarship**: a yearly national competition paying a stipend, a fee contribution and research costs, with extra fee support available for non-EU nationals. Check the current terms. [Research Ireland](https://www.researchireland.ie/funding/government-ireland-postgraduate/), accessed 2026-09-17.
- Project-funded positions are advertised by universities and research centres. EU citizens usually pay EU fees; confirm per university.

## External funding into the UK
- **Commonwealth PhD Scholarships** for candidates from eligible least developed and fragile Commonwealth countries, applied for through national nominating bodies. [British Council, Commonwealth Scholarships](https://study-uk.britishcouncil.org/scholarships-funding/commonwealth-scholarships), accessed 2026-09-17.
- The owner's home-country agencies that fund doctoral study abroad.
