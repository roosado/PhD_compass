# Referees

Letters are the item most likely to arrive late, because they depend on someone else's calendar.

## Choosing
Two or three referees who have seen the owner's research or technical work directly (thesis supervisor, project supervisor, employer in a research role) outrank better-known names who know the owner less. Record them in the Referees table.

## Asking
1. Ask early: at least 3–4 weeks before the first deadline, earlier around holidays.
2. Draft the request for the owner to send: what the owner is applying for, why this referee, the list of positions with deadlines and how each letter is submitted, and an offer to send a brag sheet.
3. Prepare the brag sheet from `assets/brag-sheet.md`: how they know the owner, concrete episodes they saw with outcomes, the qualities to emphasise with evidence, and the positions table. Use only Profile facts.
4. Record: Asked on, Agreed, Letters due for (tracker IDs and dates), Brag sheet sent.

## Keeping track
- Many portals email referees only after the owner submits or registers the referee: remind the owner to register referees early.
- One week before each deadline, check whether the portal shows the letter received; if not, draft a short, polite reminder for the owner.
- After decisions, draft a thank-you note telling each referee the outcome.

Done when every referee row has its ask date, agreement, letters due and brag-sheet status, and each letter due inside the agenda window has a reminder date.
