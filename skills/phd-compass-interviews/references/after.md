# After the interview

Capture it while memory is fresh, then follow up.

## 1. Debrief
Ask the owner, in one message:
1. Who was there (names and roles).
2. The questions asked, as close to the wording as they remember.
3. What went well, and what went badly.
4. What they learned: funding, start date, next stages, decision timeline, number of candidates.
5. Their own view now: do they still want this position, and what are their doubts?

Write the answers into the interview's **Notes** section.

## 2. Thank-you note
Draft a short email (under 120 words) for the owner to send within a day: thanks, one specific point from the conversation, anything they promised to send, and continued interest. The owner sends it.

## 3. Update the question bank
Add the questions actually asked that are not yet in `references/question-bank.md` to the save file's **Interviews** entry under "Asked", so later preparation for any target sees them. (The bank in the skill is shared and read-only; the save file is the owner's record.)

## 4. Record
- The target's data entry: `next` = expected decision (or the next stage), `nextDate` = the stated date, or two weeks later when none was stated.
- If the panel asked for anything (references, transcripts, a writing sample), add it as a dated action.
- Run `sync`.

Done when the notes, the thank-you draft, any requested items and the next date are all recorded.
