# Continental Europe

How doctoral positions are usually funded and found across EU and EEA countries and Switzerland. These are structural patterns. Salaries, stipends and deadlines change every year: look them up live from the institution or funder and cite that page.

## Routes into a PhD
1. **Advertised positions.** A PI or project hires a named doctoral candidate for a defined project, often as a fixed-term employee paid from a grant (national agency, ERC, EU project, industry). The ad names the supervisor, the funding and a closing date. Common in the Netherlands, Germany, the Nordic countries, Switzerland, Austria and Belgium.
2. **Doctoral programmes and graduate schools with calls.** Cohort recruitment with fixed deadlines: university doctoral schools, national calls, graduate schools of research institutes. Common in France and Italy, and alongside positions in Germany and elsewhere.
3. **MSCA Doctoral Networks.** EU-funded consortia that recruit a cohort of doctoral candidates across several countries after the grant is signed. Posts appear on EURAXESS and the network's website, with a mobility rule (below).
4. **Speculative contact.** Many groups hire when a new grant starts, sometimes before advertising. A strong-fit group with a young grant is worth a short, specific email even with nothing advertised.

## Eligibility traps
- **MSCA Doctoral Networks mobility rule.** Doctoral candidates must not have resided or carried out their main activity (work, studies) in the country of the recruiting institution for more than 12 months in the 36 months immediately before their recruitment date. Check it against the owner's residence history for each host country, and confirm the wording in the call. [Heidelberg University, MSCA Doctoral Networks](https://www.uni-heidelberg.de/en/research/research-service/eu-funding/horizon-europe/msca-doctoral-networks), accessed 2026-09-17.
- **Similar rules elsewhere.** The "la Caixa" Foundation INPhINIT Incoming doctoral fellowships (Spain and Portugal) exclude candidates who resided or carried out their main activity in Spain or Portugal for more than 12 months in the three years before the call's closing date. [la Caixa, INPhINIT Incoming](https://lacaixafoundation.org/en/doctoral-inphinit-fellowships-incoming-call), accessed 2026-09-17.
- **Degree timing.** Admission usually needs a completed second-cycle (Master's-level) degree or equivalent. The ad says whether it must be finished at application or at the contract start. Sweden, for example, requires a second-cycle degree, or 240 credits of which 60 are at second-cycle level, or equivalent knowledge. [Eurydice, Sweden: third-cycle programmes](https://eurydice.eacea.ec.europa.eu/national-education-systems/sweden/third-cycle-phd-programmes), accessed 2026-09-17.
- **Contract or scholarship.** An employment contract brings social security and pension rights; a scholarship or stipend often does not. Record which one the ad offers, and treat "places without a scholarship" as not funded.
- **Non-EU/EEA citizens** need a residence permit for research or study. Allow for the processing time between offer and start.
- **Language.** Many research groups work in English, but not all: check the ad. Admission may still ask for a certificate unless a degree was taught in English. Daily life may need the local language: note it for the owner.

## Country patterns
| Country | Usual model | Notes | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Netherlands | Doctoral candidates are mostly university employees on a four-year track | Ads per project on AcademicTransfer and university sites; a few "scholarship PhD" routes pay less, so check the contract type | [Eurydice, Netherlands](https://eurydice.eacea.ec.europa.eu/national-education-systems/netherlands/third-cycle-phd-programmes) |
| Germany | Two routes: an individual doctorate with a supervisor, often employed as a research associate on a part-time contract, or a structured programme (graduate school, research training group) with a contract or scholarship | Part-time contract percentages vary by field; Max Planck, Helmholtz, Leibniz and Fraunhofer institutes hire too | [Research in Germany, two ways to a PhD](https://www.research-in-germany.org/your-goal/phd/two-ways-to-get-your-phd.html); [DAAD, PhD studies](https://www.daad.de/en/studying-in-germany/phd-studies-research/) |
| France | Doctoral contracts through a doctoral school, industrial CIFRE contracts with a company, and contracts on project grants | Doctoral school competitions and project ads appear on ADUM, ABG and institution sites | [doctorat.gouv.fr, funding](https://doctorat.gouv.fr/en/before/funding/); [Campus France, financing a doctorate](https://www.campusfrance.org/en/how-to-finance-Doctorate-France) |
| Italy | Yearly public calls (bando) per university with an entry examination; programmes last at least three years | Calls list places with a scholarship (borsa) and sometimes places without one; only the former are funded | [University of Milan, doctoral scholarships](https://www.unimi.it/en/study/postgraduate-study/doctoral-research-phd-programmes/doctoral-scholarships-and-fees/doctoral-scholarships) |
| Spain, Portugal | Predoctoral contracts attached to funded projects, institutional programmes, and fellowship schemes such as INPhINIT | INPhINIT hosts are accredited centres of excellence | [la Caixa, INPhINIT](https://lacaixafoundation.org/en/doctoral-inphinit-fellowships) |
| Sweden | Doctoral studentships are employment; the degree is 240 credits (four years) | Ads on university vacancy pages | [Eurydice, Sweden](https://eurydice.eacea.ec.europa.eu/national-education-systems/sweden/third-cycle-phd-programmes) |

## Calendar patterns
- **Advertised positions** appear all year, clustered around project starts. Posts for an autumn start often appear from winter to late spring, and some appear only a few months before the start.
- **MSCA Doctoral Networks** recruit after the grant agreement is signed, roughly a year after the call closes. Find the current call's closing and results dates on the MSCA website and add them to `calls`.
- **Doctoral schools and national calls** have fixed yearly deadlines. Look them up per institution, and never infer a date from a previous year.

## External funding for incoming doctoral candidates
Look these up by the owner's citizenship and destination; each has its own eligibility:
- DAAD doctoral scholarships for Germany, aimed mainly at applicants from developing and emerging countries. [DAAD scholarships overview](https://www.daad.de/en/studying-in-germany/scholarships/daad-scholarships/), accessed 2026-09-17.
- Swiss Government Excellence Scholarships for doctoral research in Switzerland, which need a host professor's nomination. [SERI, at a glance](https://www.sbfi.admin.ch/en/swiss-government-excellence-scholarships-at-a-glance), accessed 2026-09-17.
- "la Caixa" INPhINIT (Spain, Portugal), above.
- The owner's home-country agencies that fund doctoral study abroad.
