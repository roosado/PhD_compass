# Tailored CV

A copy of the master CV reshaped for one target. Tailoring changes emphasis, order and length, never the facts.

## Steps
1. Copy the master into the target's folder under the target's file name.
2. **Map the target's needs to the owner's evidence.** List the 3–5 things the ad or group needs most (methods, systems, skills, experience) and, for each, the Profile item that shows it, or "gap".
3. **Order.** Education first while the owner is a student or recent graduate; then the section holding the strongest evidence for this target (usually research experience). Within a section, most relevant first when dates would bury it; otherwise reverse chronological.
4. **Emphasise.** Lead each relevant entry with the method or result the target cares about, using the target's terms where they truly describe the work. Add a concrete result (a number or an outcome) only if Profile holds it.
5. **Trim.** Shorten or drop entries that serve no target need, starting with the oldest and least related, until the length fits:
   - the ad's limit, if one is given;
   - otherwise two pages for positions and programmes outside North America;
   - a concise academic CV of the length the content needs for North American programmes.
6. **Profile line (optional).** A 2–3 line summary at the top only when the owner's path is not obvious from the headings (a field change, a gap). It states the direction and the strongest evidence, with no adjectives.
7. **Required extras.** Add what the ad asks for: publication list, referees' contact details, language levels, nationality or date of birth only when the ad or country norm requires it and the owner agrees. Include a photo only when the ad asks for one or the owner wants it.

## Honesty lines
- No new skills, tools, results, grades or roles. A gap stays a gap; the letter can address it.
- Respect notes in Profile about what documents must not say (for example a company the owner does not want named).
- Keep dates, titles and grades identical to the master.

Done when every target need has its evidence line or an explicit gap in your notes, the length fits, and the section 5 checks in SKILL.md pass.
