# United States and Canada

Structural patterns only. Deadlines, stipends and test requirements vary by programme and change yearly: look them up live and cite the programme's page.

## United States

### How it works
- Applicants apply to a **programme** (a department or an umbrella graduate programme) in a yearly admissions round, not usually to one advertised post. Some programmes admit students straight into a lab; others, common in the biomedical sciences, use lab rotations in the first year.
- Many programmes admit students with a bachelor's degree, and a prior Master's rarely shortens the PhD much. Check the programme's typical time to degree.
- **Funding.** In many science and engineering programmes an offer includes a tuition waiver and a stipend (fellowship, teaching or research assistantship) for several years. In other fields funding varies widely. Under a fully-funded rule, record a programme as funded only when a source states tuition and stipend coverage for admitted doctoral students, including international students.

### Eligibility traps
- **Citizenship-restricted fellowships.** Several major national fellowships, such as the NSF Graduate Research Fellowship, accept only US citizens, nationals and permanent residents. International students depend on university and department funding. [NSF GRFP eligibility](https://www.nsf.gov/funding/initiatives/grfp/eligibility), accessed 2026-09-17.
- **English tests and waivers.** Most programmes ask international applicants for TOEFL or IELTS unless a waiver applies; waiver rules differ by university.
- **Tests and fees.** Some programmes still require the GRE; many have dropped it. Application fees are charged per programme, and some offer waivers. Check each programme.
- **Visa.** The university issues the document for a student visa (F-1 or J-1) after admission, and it usually requires proof of funding.

### Calendar patterns
- Application deadlines for autumn entry fall mostly between late autumn and mid-winter; confirm each programme's date.
- Interviews and visit days follow in winter and early spring.
- **April 15 Resolution.** At signatory universities, admitted students need not accept or decline an offer of financial support before April 15. [Council of Graduate Schools, April 15 Resolution](https://cgsnet.org/resources/for-current-prospective-graduate-students/april-15-resolution), accessed 2026-09-17.

### What applications ask for
Statement of purpose, usually three recommendation letters, transcripts, CV, test scores where required. In many fields, emailing one or two faculty members whose labs fit before applying helps confirm that they are taking students.

## Canada

### How it works
- Many programmes expect the applicant to find a **supervisor** first, and some admit only with a supervisor's agreement. Funding packages come from the university, the supervisor's grants and scholarships, and vary by university and field.
- International tuition can be higher than domestic tuition; some universities offset it for doctoral students. Check the specific package.

### Eligibility traps
- **Federal doctoral scholarships.** From 2025 the tri-agency Canada Graduate Research Scholarships – Doctoral (CGRS D) replaced the Vanier CGS and the earlier doctoral awards. International students may apply only once they are registered in a doctoral programme at an eligible Canadian institution. [University of British Columbia, CGRS D](https://www.grad.ubc.ca/awards/canada-graduate-research-scholarships-doctoral-cgrs-d-program); [University of Toronto, Vanier CGS discontinued](https://www.sgs.utoronto.ca/awards/vanier-canada-graduate-scholarship/), accessed 2026-09-17.
- **Study permit.** International students need a study permit; programmes in Quebec also need a Quebec acceptance certificate (CAQ). Allow for processing time.

## External funding into North America
- **Fulbright Foreign Student Program** (to the US): applications go through the binational Fulbright Commission or the US Embassy in the applicant's home country. [Fulbright Foreign Student Program](https://foreign.fulbrightonline.org/about/foreign-student-program), accessed 2026-09-17.
- The owner's home-country agencies that fund doctoral study abroad.
